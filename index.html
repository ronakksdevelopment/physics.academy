How a class runs

  • Concept first. Every topic starts with a real-world question before any formula.
  • Demonstrations and simple experiments to see the idea at work.
  • Graded problem sets, from NCERT basics to JEE and NEET level.
  • Doubt time at the end of every session, not a week later.

A physics academy built around understanding, not memorising

Innovative Physics Academy is a coaching institute in Agartala dedicated to one subject. Students who learn physics from the ground up stop fearing numericals, and that confidence carries into board exams and competitive tests.

We teach school boards (CBSE, ICSE and Tripura Board), JEE Main and Advanced, NEET and first year university physics. Batches are kept small so every student is seen, heard and corrected early.
